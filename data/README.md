# Data

Large data is not committed. Keep it on the team's shared drive.

- `trajectories/`: ground-truth logs exported from Unity (id, frame, x, y)
- `results/`: small summary CSVs used for the paper figures (these may be committed)

## Jülich pedestrian data archive

Real trajectories from the Jülich bottleneck experiments: http://ped.fz-juelich.de/da
Download into `data/julich/` (ignored by git). Check each dataset's licence and terms of use,
and cite Boltes et al. (2018).
